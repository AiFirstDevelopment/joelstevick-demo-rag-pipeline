**book-21** Why does the book say a twenty-round agent loop costs about twenty times a single request?

> The book says the opposite: a twenty-round loop over a growing transcript does not cost twenty times one request, it costs closer to two hundred. The reason is that the entire conversation is re-sent on every turn, so cost is quadratic in turn count.
