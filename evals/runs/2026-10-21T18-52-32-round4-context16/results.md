**book-19** Why does the book recommend having the model grade its own work as the main quality check?

> The book recommends the opposite: its rule is that nothing grades its own work, not the model and not a second call to it. A model asked whether its own work is correct says yes either way, so the answer tells you nothing. A grader whose only input is the thing being graded is "a text generator with a strong prior towards approval." What works instead is a check whose outcome does not depend on the model's opinion of its own output.


**book-20** Why does the book say temperature 0 makes the model fully deterministic?

> It doesn't. The book says temperature 0 makes only the selection rule deterministic, because the most likely candidate is taken every time. The model itself is not made deterministic: identical requests at temperature zero can still produce different text, for reasons in floating-point arithmetic and server-side batching that are outside your control.
